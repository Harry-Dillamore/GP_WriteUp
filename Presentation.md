# **Card Creator Tool**

- The purpose of this tool is allow people to create new cards for the game in a way that is **standardised automatically**.

![bg right:40%](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/42/Playing_cards_collage.jpg/250px-Playing_cards_collage.jpg)

---

# **Justification**

- Creating new cards is **largely automated**, with *minimal onboarding* required.
- All cards can be referenced in a **standardised way**, with predefined *properties and variables*.
- Reduces **manual work** and ensures *consistency*.

![bg right:40%  right:50%](https://media1.tenor.com/m/--LpnY9zae0AAAAd/automatica-robot.gif)

---

# **Standardisation**

- The tool creates new card assets based on **custom templates**.
- Cards maintain a **consistent data structure** as they are children of the same *blueprints*.
- An *empty blueprint* is connected to each card asset, providing a container for **unique card logic**.

![bg right:40%](https://media1.tenor.com/m/mzSnq2Onb4EAAAAC/human-transformation.gif)

---

# **How it works**

- An **Editor Utility Widget** provides a user-friendly form for defining *card properties*.
- The form includes **standard properties** (e.g., *Name, Cost, Rarity*) required for every card.

---

# **Further Development**

- The tool is designed for **extensibility**, allowing for easy addition or adjustment of *properties*.
- Currently developing **custom C++ nodes** for common actions to streamline the *blueprint workflow*.
