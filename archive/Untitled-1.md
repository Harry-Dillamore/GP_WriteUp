## **01 – Theoretical Toolkit: The Definitions**

- **Procedural Rhetoric:** The art of making an argument through **processes** (rules and code) rather than images or words. You aren't _telling_ the player the system is rigged; your code is _forcing_ them to experience it.
- **Ludological:** Derived from "Ludology," the academic study of games. A "ludological analysis" focuses on the **mechanics** and rules rather than the story or art.
- [cite_start]**Anthropomorphism:** The attribution of human characteristics, emotions, or intentions to non-human entities, like your "Business Pigs"[cite: 16].
- **Urban/Wilderness Binary:** The theoretical divide between "corrupt" cities and "pure" nature. In your game, the penthouse is a site where the city has completely swallowed the natural world.
- **Pastoral Fantasy:** A romanticized, unrealistic view of rural life. Your pigs subvert this by being greedy corporate agents instead of "happy farm animals".
- **Neoliberalism:** An economic ideology that prioritizes competition, deregulation, and the "market" as the only measure of value. [cite_start]This is the "brutal honesty" of New Trotter[cite: 7].
- [cite_start]**Systemic Erasure of Agency:** When a system (like your Bankruptcy code) is designed to slowly take away a person's ability to make choices or act freely [cite: 95-96].

- [cite_start]**Greedy Piggies Application:** \* The **Audit System** forces players to choose between the "truth" and the "claim," making distrust a mechanical requirement for play [cite: 73-76].
