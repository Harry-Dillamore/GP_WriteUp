Assessment Rubric and Learning Outcome Mapping
Assessment Rubric and Learning Outcome Mapping


Learning Outcome
	

Summary

LO1
	

Critical, contextual and academic understanding of practice, supported by research and analysis

LO2
	

Understanding and application of tools and programming using industry-standard software

LO3
	

Application of technical and professional skills within a collaborative production context


Professionalism (20%)

Primary LO Mapping: LO3

Secondary Mapping: LO2

Grade Band
	

Descriptor

0–24 Inadequate
	

Work is poorly presented or incomplete. Little to no evidence of industry-standard workflows. Disorganised project structure, inconsistent naming conventions, unreadable or missing documentation.

25–39 Weak
	

Basic attempt at professional presentation but with frequent issues. Limited use of industry-standard workflows. Documentation lacks clarity, structure, or consistency.

40–59 Satisfactory
	

Work is presented clearly with generally appropriate workflows. Project structure and naming conventions are mostly consistent. Documentation is readable but descriptive rather than reflective.

60–69 Strong
	

Clear and well-structured presentation following industry-standard workflows. Good project organisation, consistent naming, and clear documentation that supports the development process.

70–79 Excellent
	

Highly professional presentation. Strong adherence to industry conventions and workflows. Documentation is clear, structured, and supports decision-making and production processes.

80+ Professional Quality
	

Industry-ready standard. Exemplary project organisation, naming, workflow, and documentation quality. Reflects professional studio practice and could be handed to another developer with minimal onboarding.

Learning Outcome Alignment

    LO3: Demonstrates professional practice, responsibility, and production standards
    LO2: Uses appropriate tools and workflows expected in industry contexts

Research (30%)

Primary LO Mapping: LO1

Secondary Mapping: LO3

Grade Band
	

Descriptor

0–24 Inadequate
	

Little or no research evident. Sources are missing, inappropriate, or unreferenced. No meaningful analysis or application to the work.

25–39 Weak
	

Limited research using a small range of sources. Minimal critical analysis. Research is largely descriptive and weakly connected to the outcome.

40–59 Satisfactory
	

Adequate range of credible sources. Research is referenced correctly and used to inform decisions, though analysis may be surface-level or inconsistent.

60–69 Strong
	

Good quality and variety of academic, industry, and practice-based sources. Clear analysis and justification of decisions. Evidence of research-informed iteration.

70–79 Excellent
	

Strong critical engagement with research. Excellent integration of theory, industry examples, and practice. Clear evidence of data collection, analysis, and implementation.

80+ Professional Quality
	

Outstanding research depth and critical analysis. Academic conventions are applied rigorously. Research drives innovation, decision-making, and evaluation at a professional level.

Learning Outcome Alignment

    LO1: Critical and contextual understanding supported by academic research
    LO3: Evidence-based decision-making within a development process

Creativity (20%)

Primary LO Mapping: LO1

Secondary Mapping: LO2

Grade Band
	

Descriptor

0–24 Inadequate
	

Work shows no originality. Heavily derivative or copied solutions with no adaptation or reflection.

25–39 Weak
	

Limited creative engagement. Solutions rely heavily on existing examples with minimal experimentation or innovation.

40–59 Satisfactory
	

Some original ideas and problem-solving approaches. Combines existing methods with minor adaptations to meet requirements.

60–69 Strong
	

Clear evidence of creative thinking and problem-solving. Research-informed experimentation and thoughtful combination of ideas.

70–79 Excellent
	

Highly original and well-considered creative solutions. Demonstrates confidence in experimentation, iteration, and design decision-making.

80+ Professional Quality
	

Exceptional creativity comparable to industry or independent professional work. Innovative methodologies and advanced ideas are fully realised and justified.

Learning Outcome Alignment

    LO1: Critical application of ideas and contextual understanding
    LO2: Creative use of tools and technical approaches

Technical (30%)

Primary LO Mapping: LO2

Secondary Mapping: LO3

Grade Band
	

Descriptor

0–24 Inadequate
	

Work is technically incomplete or non-functional. Major bugs prevent core functionality. Poor execution or failure to produce a viable build.

25–39 Weak
	

Basic functionality present but with frequent bugs or technical issues. Limited understanding of tools or programming concepts.

40–59 Satisfactory
	

Functional outcome with manageable issues. Demonstrates a basic understanding of tools and programming, though implementation may lack polish or optimisation.

60–69 Strong
	

Solid technical execution with minimal issues. Good understanding and application of tools, programming concepts, and debugging processes.

70–79 Excellent
	

High-quality, stable implementation. Well-structured, efficient, and robust code or systems. Evidence of thorough testing and optimisation.

80+ Professional Quality
	

Industry-ready technical execution. Clean, maintainable, and scalable implementation with rigorous testing and professional build quality.

Learning Outcome Alignment

    LO2: Competent and confident use of industry-standard tools and programming
    LO3: Technical contribution to shared outcomes and production quality
